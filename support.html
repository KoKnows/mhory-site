Support

Email info@mhory.ai and a person will reply. Include your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Something's wrong with sign-in or Plus? Tell us the email you signed in with and whether you used Apple or Google. We can't see your password (we never have it) but we can see your account's plan and fix it from our side.

Common questions

The app says it can't find my location.
Mhory needs location access "while using the app". Check Settings › Privacy › Location Services (iPhone) or Settings › Location (Android) and make sure Mhory is allowed. Indoors, GPS can take a minute; the map's press-and-hold lets you pick a spot by hand in the meantime.
Nothing shows up for where I am.
Some places have little recorded history online, especially outside the US and UK. Widen the search radius in Settings, or press and hold on the map to look at a nearby town.
The summary or chat says I've used my allowance.
Free accounts get a monthly allowance of summaries and chat messages, which resets each month. Mhory Plus raises the limits. History, the timeline and the map never count against it.
I subscribed but the app still shows the free plan.
Open the Mhory Plus screen and tap Restore purchases. If it still shows the free plan, email us with the email you signed in with.
How do I cancel Mhory Plus?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not in the app. On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions. You keep Plus until the end of the paid period.
The summary got something wrong.
Summaries are AI-written from public records and can misread them. Every timeline item links to its source; if the source itself is wrong, the fix belongs there (on Wikipedia or OpenStreetMap, say). If Mhory misread a correct source, email us the place and what it got wrong so we can improve it.
Where are my trail marks kept?
On your phone only. They aren't uploaded, so they don't move to a new phone and are gone if you delete the app.
How do I delete my account?
Open the Account screen in the app and tap Delete account. It takes effect immediately. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we'll delete it within 30 days. Deleting the account doesn't cancel a subscription; do that in your App Store or Google Play settings. See the Privacy Policy for what's deleted.
