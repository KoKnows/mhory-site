Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026

This policy is for the Mhory app ("Mhory", "we", "us"). This policy explains what the Mhory app does with your information. The short version: your location is used to look up history and is not stored by us; your account holds only what's needed to run it; your trail marks stay on your phone.

1. Your location

Mhory's whole purpose is to tell you about the place you're in, so it asks for your location while you use the app. It never tracks you in the background.

To find history nearby, the app sends coordinates (or the spot you picked on the map) to the public services that hold the records:

  • Wikipedia and Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation), for articles and dated facts near you
  • OpenStreetMap and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation), for the map, place names and historic features
  • The US National Park Service, for the National Register of Historic Places
  • Historic England, for the National Heritage List for England

Each of these receives the coordinates needed to answer the request, together with a note identifying the app, as their usage policies require. They operate under their own privacy policies. We do not send them your name, account or anything else about you.

We do not keep a record of where you have been. The app caches the history it finds on your phone for up to seven days so it doesn't have to fetch it again; you can clear that cache in Settings.

2. AI summaries and chat

The written summary and the Ask feature are generated by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. To produce them, the app sends Anthropic the history it gathered about the place (the "dossier"), the place name, and, for Ask, the questions you type. Anthropic processes this to generate the response. Anthropic's handling of that data is described in its privacy policy.

When you're signed in, these requests go through our own server first, which checks your plan and passes the request on. Our server keeps a copy of generated summaries, keyed by the history they were written from, so that the next person standing in the same place gets the same summary without a new AI call. These cached summaries contain no information about you. We do not store your chat questions.

If you choose to use your own Anthropic API key under Settings › Advanced, the app talks to Anthropic directly and our server is not involved. Your key is stored in your phone's secure keychain and never sent to us.

3. Your account

You can use the History and Map features without an account. Signing in is needed for the AI summary and Ask, so that we can provide a free allowance and paid plans without you needing an API key.

You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive the email address and name they share with you at sign-in (Apple lets you hide your email, and that works with Mhory). We use this to identify your account and, if you write to us, to reply.

For each account we store:

4. Subscriptions

Mhory Plus is sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play. They handle payment, and we never see your card details. They tell us only whether your subscription is active and when it expires. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

5. Your trail

The marks you leave at places, and any notes you write with them, are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics trackers in the app.
  • We don't track your location in the background or build a history of where you've been.
  • We don't use your questions or your location to train AI models.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from the Account screen in the app; it takes effect immediately. This removes your profile, plan and usage records from our servers. Cached summaries are not linked to accounts and are unaffected. Your trail marks live on your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

If you can't get into the app, email info@mhory.ai from the address you signed in with and we'll delete the account within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel that in your App Store or Google Play settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to how it's used. Email us and we'll help. If you're in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Mhory is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll remove it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll let you know in the app and update the date at the top of this page.
